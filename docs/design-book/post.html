Squishy Post Office

A cheerful yellow post office where all the letters in town get sorted. Har Gow (the letter-writing dumpling) is its biggest customer.

  • Address: 3 Butter Boulevard
  • Unlocks: Paw Palace reaches level 3
  • Minigame: Sort-a-Lot
  • Play time: Endless (3 hearts)

Try itDrag the slider to see the building at every level. Level 0 is the empty plot it starts as.

The minigame

Sort-a-Lot

▶ Play the prototypeWorks on phone and computer. Each minigame is one file you can open on its own: play/sort-a-lot.html

A quick swipe-sorting game. Letters ride along a conveyor belt. Each has a coloured stamp that matches one of the family mail bags. Swipe each letter into the right bag before it falls off the end of the belt.

How it plays

  1. Letters roll along the conveyor belt from left to right.
  2. Each letter has a coloured stamp matching one of the mail bags (each bag has a family squishy on it).
  3. Swipe or drag a letter down into the bag with the same stamp. A quick flick toward a bag is enough.
  4. A letter in the wrong bag, or one that falls off the end of the belt, costs a heart.
  5. 3 hearts and the post office closes. Otherwise the letters never stop.

Controls

Swipe. That’s it. Great for the youngest players. PC: drag with the mouse, or press 1-6 to send the front letter to that bag.

Scoring

  • +10 per letter, × your streak multiplier (sort 5 in a row for ×2, up to ×5).
  • Your best score is saved.

It never ends, it just gets harder

  • Every 7 letters the belt speeds up and letters come closer together.
  • A new bag opens every couple of levels, up to 6 bags.
  • Later, some stamps get white spots: a spotty pink stamp goes in the spotty pink bag, not the plain one.

Squishy helpers

You always play with squishies you own. Some families are extra good at this game:

  • Dumpling Gang sorters slow the belt down by 15%.
  • Pet Pals sorters auto-sort one letter every 10 seconds (they fetch it!).

Rewards

Coins equal to the points ÷ 55 (no milestone bonus). About 35 coins per minute for a good player. Sorted letters sometimes contain a decor item for a house.

Keep going (optional ad)

When the run ends, the player can choose to watch a short ad once per run: carry on with 1 heart, and the letters past the middle are cleared away. “No thanks” ends the run as normal. See the ad rules.

Building levels

From empty plot to level 10

The plot is fixed on the map and shows a “Build me!” sign until you pay to build level 1. After that, each upgrade unlocks when your best score in Sort-a-Lot is high enough, and then costs coins. Better results in the minigame = a fancier building. Tap any level to see it bigger.

  1. Mail kiosk. A teal-striped kiosk with an envelope sign.
  2. Kiosk + board. A board for parcels waiting.
  3. Little post office. A yellow building with a gable roof.
  4. Red post box. A post box out front and awnings.
  5. Wider office. Big windows and a bench.
  6. Second floor. A sorting room upstairs.
  7. Delivery bike. A bike with a parcel crate and Har Gow.
  8. Lit sign & garden. Bulbs, flowers and fence.
  9. Paper plane vane. A paper-plane weather vane on the roof.
  10. Golden Post. Flags, star medal and sparkles.
