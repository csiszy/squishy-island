Splash Squad Station

A fire station for the bravest squishies. There are no scary fires on Squishy Island: the firefighters spend their days getting the coloured water ready for the big Splash Parade, sorting every colour into its own bucket. It grows from a little hose cart into a big station with a fire truck and a hose tower.

  • Address: 5 Squeeze Street
  • Unlocks: Open from day one: an empty plot, build it with coins
  • Minigame: Bucket Brigade (level-based puzzle)
  • Play time: Level-based (1–3 min per level)

The minigame

Bucket Brigade

▶ Play the prototypeWorks on phone and computer. Each minigame is one file you can open on its own: play/bucket-brigade.html

A level-based colour-sorting puzzle (like the “water sort” games). The coloured water for the Splash Parade got all mixed up between the fire buckets! Pour from bucket to bucket until every bucket holds just one colour. Fewer moves means more stars, and every win unlocks the next level.

How it plays

  1. Each bucket holds 4 layers of coloured water (5 in some later levels). There are always 2 empty buckets to help.
  2. Tap a bucket to pick it up, then tap another bucket to pour into it. The bucket flies over, tips and pours.
  3. You can only pour onto the same colour, or into an empty bucket, and only while there is room. All the matching layers on top pour together.
  4. When a bucket is full of one colour it gets a gold rim and a little firefighter squishy pops on top. Sort every colour to win.
  5. From level 8, some layers are smoky (grey with a “?”): you only see their colour when you uncover them.
  6. Helpers: Undo (free, but the move still counts), Restart, and + Bucket (one extra empty bucket per level; using it caps that level at 2 stars).

Controls

Tap a bucket, then another. Every colour also has its own little symbol (dot, triangle, star, leaf…) so colour-blind players can play too. PC: click, or ← → and Space, U to undo.

Scoring

  • Every level is checked by a built-in solver when it is made, so it can always be solved, and the solver sets the 3-star move target.
  • 3 stars: finish within the target. 2 stars: within the target + about 30%. 1 star: any finish. Smoky levels get 2 extra moves on the target.
  • The top panel shows how many stars you would get if you finished right now.

Levels that never run out

  • Level 1 starts with 3 colours. Every 2 levels add a colour, up to 10 colours (12 buckets) by level 15.
  • From level 8, smoky hidden layers appear, more and more of them (up to 60% of the hidden-able layers).
  • From level 18, every 3rd level uses tall 5-layer buckets.
  • Every 10th level is a Big Level with double coins.

Squishy helpers

You always play with squishies you own. Some families are extra good at this game:

  • Sea Squish firefighters give you a second free + Bucket per level.
  • Sky Puffs clear the smoke from one bucket at the start of each level.

Rewards

Coins only when you beat a level: the first time, 30 + 15 per star (45–75 coins; Big Levels pay double). Replaying pays 10 coins + 15 for each new star you didn't have before. The building's upgrades unlock as you beat more levels.

Building levels

From empty plot to level 10

The plot is fixed on the map and shows a “Build me!” sign until you pay to build level 1. After that, each upgrade unlocks when you have beaten enough levels of Bucket Brigade, and then costs coins. Better results in the minigame = a fancier building. Tap any level to see it bigger.

  1. Hose cart. A red cart with a flame sign.
  2. Cart + board. A chalkboard and plant.
  3. Little station. A garage door and red trim.
  4. Awnings. Red awnings over the windows.
  5. Wider station. Big windows by the garage.
  6. Second floor. Bunk room upstairs.
  7. Fire truck. A cute red truck with a ladder parks outside.
  8. Lit sign & garden. Bulbs, flowers and fence.
  9. Hose tower. A tall red tower to dry the hoses.
  10. Golden Station. Flags, star medal and sparkles.
