Squishy Factory

The factory is where coins become squishies. Buy a Mystery Box, watch it ride the conveyor belt and pop open to reveal a random squishy. Rarer squishies are much harder to get. Next door is the Gem Shop, where the Legendaries and two shop-only squishies are sold directly (no luck involved) for Squish Gems.

  • Address: 4 Cloudberry Hill
  • Unlocks: Free from the start
  • Pays with: coins (boxes), Squish Gems (Gem Shop), or a short ad (up to 3 free boxes a day)
  • Levels: grow with boxes opened

Kind rules so it never feels unfair

  • Rare guarantee: if 9 boxes in a row give nothing Rare or better, the 10th box is always Rare or better. The bar at the top of the box screen shows how close you are.
  • Every box wins something. A new squishy moves into its house. A squishy you already have goes onto your Spare Shelf (see the guide): use it to level up that squishy, trade it at the Market, or swap it for Squish Stars (Common 5, Uncommon 15, Rare 40, Epic 100, Legendary 300).
  • Sparkly pulls: 1 box in 50 gives a Sparkly version of the squishy (same squishy, shimmering coat and its own fanfare). Sparklies fill a second page of the collection book.
  • Star Shop: spend Stars to pick any Common or Uncommon you are missing, or special decor.
  • Boxes are never sold for Squish Gems or real money. They cost coins (which only come from playing) or a short, optional ad. The odds above are shown on the box screen before every box, including the free one.
  • Pacing: every minigame pays about 35 coins per minute of good play, so a Mystery Box is about 6 minutes of play and a Golden Box about half an hour. The exact coin rule for every minigame is in the guide.

Premium currency

The Gem Shop

A counter on the factory’s ground floor sells single squishies for Squish Gems. You always know exactly what you get: nothing in the Gem Shop is random. It sells every Legendary plus two Gem Shop exclusives that are never inside any box. How Gems are earned is in the guide; a player who does the daily Gem Challenge most days can afford a Legendary about every 2–3 months without paying.

Demo wallet: 650 Gems · get more in the Gem Mine

Gem Shop rules

  • Legendaries cost 300 Gems, exclusives 500. A Legendary bought here is the same squishy as one from a box: it moves into its family house and counts for house levels.
  • Exclusives (Prism Mochi, Starlight Bun) have their own rarity colour, are not part of the 48-squishy box collection, and choose which house to visit. They can’t be traded, so they never put a real-money price on the Market.
  • One of each. Each shop squishy can be bought once. Buying a Legendary you already own is blocked (the button says “You have it”).
  • “Are you sure?” Every Gem purchase shows a pop-up with the squishy, the price and the Gems left. Switching it off is a grown-up setting. Try it on the cards above.
  • One Legendary a week is on the front shelf at 250 Gems, so there is a reason to peek in.
  • Gems are bought with real money only in the Gem Mine, always through a grown-up gate, and every Gem pack shows its price in real money. In the shop, each item also shows what it would cost in money (for example “300 Gems ≈ €2.99”, whatever the final prices are).

What the player sees

The box screen

Tapping the factory on the map opens the box screen. The player picks a box, taps Open!, and the box rides along the conveyor belt, wiggles, and bursts open with light rays in the rarity colour. Epic and Legendary pulls get a longer, sparklier reveal and a little fanfare.

Right after the reveal, the new squishy waves goodbye and the camera flies to its house on the city map so the player sees it move in and start its idle animation.

How the factory levels up

The factory grows as you open more boxes in total (it is not a minigame building). New levels unlock new box types and make the reveal fancier: level 3 adds Family Boxes, level 6 Golden Boxes, level 9 a golden box sits on the roof, and level 10 makes the chimney puff rainbow hearts.

Building levels

From little workshop to candy factory

  1. Workshop. A tiny shed with one box on the step.
  2. Loading dock. Chimney puffs and a stack of boxes.
  3. Factory hall. A sawtooth roof and a big loading door. Family Boxes unlock.
  4. Conveyor. Boxes roll out on a conveyor belt.
  5. Squishy sign. A giant squeezy squishy sign on the roof.
  6. Second floor. Bigger factory. Golden Boxes unlock.
  7. Candy chimney. A striped smokestack that puffs pink hearts.
  8. Gear room. Spinning gears in a side annex.
  9. Golden box. A golden box on the roof and fairy lights.
  10. Rainbow Factory. Rainbow heart puffs, flags and medal.
