Fun building · minigame

Squish Patrol Station

A friendly police station that looks after Squishy Island’s Lost & Found. Squishies lose balloons, mittens and keys all the time, and the officers sort the jumbled lost-property box into neat matching rows. It grows from a kiosk into a station with a patrol car and a flashing siren.

  • Address: 2 Butter Boulevard
  • Unlocks: Sweet Shoppe reaches level 3
  • Minigame: Lost & Found (level-based puzzle)
  • Play time: Level-based (1–3 min per level)

The minigame

Lost & Found

▶ Play the prototypeWorks on phone and computer. Each minigame is one file you can open on its own: play/lost-and-found.html

A level-based match-3 puzzle (Candy Crush style). The station's lost-property box tipped over and everything is jumbled! Swap neighbouring items to line up 3 or more of a kind. Every level has its own goal and a limited number of moves; beat it to earn 1–3 stars and unlock the next level.

How it plays

  1. The board is a 7 × 8 grid of lost items: balloons, mittens, candies, keys, clovers and bows. Some levels have holes that make fun shapes.
  2. Swipe an item into its neighbour (or tap one, then the other) to swap them. The swap only counts if it makes a line of 3 or more.
  3. Matched items vanish, the ones above fall down, and new ones drop in. Chain reactions score more.
  4. Each level shows its goal at the top: collect a number of certain items, find every paw-print clue (match on top of the paw prints, some need two matches), or pop all the bubble wrap (wrapped items can't be swapped; match them to pop the wrap).
  5. Reach the goal before the moves run out to win. Spare moves turn into bonus points. Out of moves = try again (no coins).
  6. Specials: 4 in a row makes a Siren (clears its row or column), an L or T shape makes a Bubble Bomb (clears around it), 5 in a row makes a Gold Badge (swap it with any item to clear every item of that kind). Swap two specials together for a mega combo.

Controls

Swipe or tap. Stuck for a few seconds? Two items wiggle to show a possible move. If no move is left, the board shuffles itself. PC: click, or arrow keys + Space.

Scoring

  • Beating the goal = 1 star. A score bar with two star markers shows the 2- and 3-star scores.
  • +30 per item (more in chain reactions), +50 per bubble wrap popped, +60 per paw print found, +300 per spare move.
  • Stars per level are saved, and you can replay any level to get more.

Levels that never run out

  • Levels 1–8 teach one thing at a time: collecting, paw prints, bubble wrap, then mixed goals.
  • After that, levels keep coming forever: bigger goals, 6 kinds of items instead of 5, board shapes with holes, and two-layer paw prints and bubble wrap.
  • Every level number is the same for every player (it is built from the level number), so friends can compare stars.
  • Every 10th level is a Big Level: a few more moves, a bigger goal, and double coins.

Squishy helpers

You always play with squishies you own. Some families are extra good at this game:

  • Pet Pals as your detective start each level with one Siren already on the board.
  • Rarer detectives get +1 / +2 / +3 extra moves (Rare / Epic / Legendary).

Rewards

Coins only when you beat a level: the first time, 30 + 15 per star (45–75 coins; Big Levels pay double). Replaying pays 10 coins + 15 for each new star you didn't have before. The building's upgrades unlock as you beat more levels. Solved cases collect into a sticker book in the station.

Keep going (optional ad)

When the run ends, the player can choose to watch a short ad once per run: get 5 more moves for the same case. “No thanks” ends the run as normal. See the ad rules.

Building levels

From empty plot to level 10

The plot is fixed on the map and shows a “Build me!” sign until you pay to build level 1. After that, each upgrade unlocks when you have beaten enough levels of Lost & Found, and then costs coins. Better results in the minigame = a fancier building. Tap any level to see it bigger.

  1. Patrol kiosk. A blue-and-white booth with a badge sign.
  2. Kiosk + board. A notice board for missing-cookie posters.
  3. Small station. A one-room station with a mansard roof.
  4. Awnings. Blue awnings over the windows.
  5. Wider station. Big windows and a sturdier front.
  6. Second floor. An upstairs office.
  7. Patrol car. A cute patrol car parked outside.
  8. Lit sign & garden. Bulbs around the sign, flowers and fence.
  9. Siren light. A red and blue light flashes on the roof.
  10. Golden Station. Flags, star medal and sparkles.
