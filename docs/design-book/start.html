First launch · story · tutorial · help

The first five minutes

What a child sees from the very first tap: a short story that explains why the town is broken, a name, and a hands-on tour where Bao Bun shows them everything by letting them do it. After that, a little i button on the map replays any part of the tour, any time.

▶ Play the opening on the mapThe design-book map plays the real story and then the tour, which leaves the map to open your free box in the Factory and to play Sky Bounce, and brings you back each time. It also plays automatically the first time someone opens the map. Turn the sound on.

Opening story

Storyboard

The story is played on the real 3D map: the camera flies between places while the town changes from golden to broken. It needs no extra art except the storm overlay, and it shows children the town they are about to fix.

Story rules

  • Skip sits in the top-right corner from the very first frame, always visible, one tap (no “are you sure?”).
  • Tapping anywhere finishes the current line; tapping again goes to the next scene. Otherwise each scene moves on by itself 2.5 s after the line ends.
  • Every line is read by a narrator voice (warm and slow) and shown as subtitles in a storybook strip, so children who can’t read yet still follow.
  • No scene is scary: the storm is purple, not black; the thunder is far away; nobody is hurt, the squishies are just blown away and can come back.
  • With Reduced animations: cuts instead of camera flights, no flash.
  • Plays once, on the very first launch. Replay from Settings → Help, or the i menu.

Sound and music

  • Music: “Once Upon a Squish” (music box and soft pad, minor key turning major at the end). Hear it on the Sound page.
  • Scene 3: wind builds up, then a far-away thunder rumble.
  • Scene 4: a soft “poof” and a white flash as the town breaks.
  • Scene 6: magic sparkle on the Factory.
  • Narration ducks the music to 35%.
  • “Let’s go!” fades the story music into the city theme.

Tour rules

  • The real screens, with everything except the target dimmed. Taps outside the target do nothing during a “do this” step.
  • If nothing happens for 5 seconds, a hand pointer shows the gesture. After 15 seconds Bao repeats the line.
  • Bao’s lines are short (max ~15 words), voiced, and always on screen as text.
  • Progress saves after each step; closing the app mid-tour resumes at the same step.
  • The tour can’t be failed and never asks for money, Gems or ads.
  • Starting money: 200 coins before the tour, about 450 after it (enough for a second box or the next house level).

The i button: help any time

  • Top-left corner of the map, the same place on every screen. On the map it opens a menu of short topics; on any other screen it jumps straight to that screen’s topic.
  • Topics (2–5 steps each): The full tour · Moving around · Houses · Minigames & coins · Mystery Boxes · Gems · Quests & visitor · Trading · Settings & saving · Watch the story.
  • Help topics use the same Bao bubble and spotlight, but every step can be skipped and nothing is spent.
  • Try it on the map: it’s the round i button in the top-left corner.
