Fun building · minigame

Bounce Park

A playground that grows one piece of equipment at a time: a sandbox first, then a seesaw, slide, swings, a climbing dome, a castle tower, a trampoline, a zip line and finally a rainbow gate.

  • Address: 3 Squeeze Street
  • Unlocks: Free from the start (first minigame)
  • Minigame: Sky Bounce
  • Play time: Endless (until you fall)

Try itDrag the slider to see the building at every level. Level 0 is the empty plot it starts as.

The minigame

Sky Bounce

▶ Play the prototypeWorks on phone and computer. Each minigame is one file you can open on its own: play/sky-bounce.html

An endless bouncing game in the style of Doodle Jump. Your squishy bounces upward by itself from cloud to cloud. Steer left and right to land on the next one and climb as high as you can.

How it plays

  1. Your squishy bounces automatically every time it lands on a cloud or leaf.
  2. Steer left and right to land on the next platform. Going off one side of the screen brings you back on the other.
  3. Collect coins on the way up. Springs launch you extra high, and a balloon floats you up for a few seconds.
  4. Crumbly grey clouds break after one bounce, and some leaves slide sideways.
  5. Missing every platform and falling off the bottom ends the run.

Controls

Phone: hold or slide a finger on the side you want to go (the real app can also offer tilt steering). PC: arrow keys or A / D.

Scoring

  • Score is the height reached in metres. A dashed line shows your best height so you can try to beat it.
  • Coins collected are kept even when you fall.

It never ends, it just gets harder

  • The higher you climb, the bigger the gaps between platforms.
  • Moving leaves, crumbly clouds and fewer safe clouds appear more and more.
  • The sky changes colour as you go: pink sunset, then a starry night.

Squishy helpers

You always play with squishies you own. Some families are extra good at this game:

  • Sky Puffs fall 25% slower (floaty!).
  • Sugar Squad bounce 10% higher.
  • Rarer squishies start with a balloon already attached (Epic, Legendary).

Rewards

You keep every coin you collect on the way up (coins now appear a bit more often), plus a milestone bonus of +10 / +25 / +50 coins when a run passes 600 / 1,200 / 2,000 m (you get the highest one reached). About 35 coins per minute for a good player.

Keep going (optional ad)

When the run ends, the player can choose to watch a short ad once per run: a balloon floats your squishy back up into the clouds. “No thanks” ends the run as normal. See the ad rules.

Building levels

From empty plot to level 10

The plot is fixed on the map and shows a “Build me!” sign until you pay to build level 1. After that, each upgrade unlocks when your best score in Sky Bounce is high enough, and then costs coins. Better results in the minigame = a fancier building. Tap any level to see it bigger.

  1. Sandbox. A sandbox with a bucket and spade.
  2. Seesaw. A mint seesaw rocks up and down.
  3. Little slide. A ladder and a pink slide.
  4. Swing set. Two swings, one already in use.
  5. Spring rider. A yellow duck on a spring.
  6. Climbing dome. An orange climbing frame dome.
  7. Castle tower. The slide becomes a castle tower with a flag.
  8. Trampoline. Plus a garden, fence and lamps.
  9. Zip line. A zip line from the tower top to a far pole.
  10. Rainbow Park. A giant rainbow gate and sparkles.
