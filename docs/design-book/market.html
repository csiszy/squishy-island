Squishy shop · trade with other players

Swap Meet Market

The second way to get squishies. You are paired with a random player, you each put squishies on the table, and you haggle with three big buttons until you both accept. Only spares from your Spare Shelf can be traded, so the squishy living in your house never leaves.

  • Address: 4 Squeeze Street
  • Unlocks: Open from day one: an empty plot, build it with coins
  • Trade size: up to 4 squishies per side
  • Levels: grow with trades finished

How trading works

Plus, cross, tick

  1. Find a partner. Tap Trade now and the game pairs you with a random player who is also looking to trade. You see their name, their town flag and their squishy collection.
  2. Build your offers. Tap squishies in your collection to put them on the table. Your partner does the same. Both offers update live.
  3. Haggle with three buttons:
    • + Ask for more sends a bouncing "a bit more please?" bubble. Your partner can add something or reply with a "that’s my best" emote.
    • ✕ Decline ends the trade politely and you can look for a new partner.
    • ✓ Accept locks your side. When both players have accepted, a 3-second countdown starts (either player can still cancel), then the squishies swap.
  4. Moving day. The spares you traded away wave goodbye from the shelf. The new ones arrive in a little moving truck: a squishy you didn’t have moves into its house, one you already had goes onto your Spare Shelf.

The unfair-trade alert

Every squishy has a hidden trade value based on rarity (Common 1, Uncommon 3, Rare 8, Epic 20, Legendary 60). The trade screen does not show any fairness meter while you haggle: players judge the offers themselves.

Only if you press the tick on a trade that is significantly unfair to you, meaning you give more than twice the value you receive, a big red alert pops up: "Whoa! This trade is much better for them than for you." It shows both totals and asks you to tap Go back or Trade anyway. The alert only protects the player who is losing out; it never blocks a trade.

Keeping it safe for kids

Trading rules

  • No chat. Players can only use the three buttons and a small set of friendly emotes (wave, heart, thinking, "best offer").
  • No coins or real money in trades. Only squishies can be swapped, so nobody can be pressured into paying.
  • Undo window: a finished trade can be reversed within 10 minutes if both players agree.
  • Daily limit of 10 trades, and the last squishy of each family in your city can’t be traded away (so a house never becomes empty by accident).
  • Parent switch: trading can be turned off or set to "friends only" in parental settings.

What the player sees

The trade screen

The trade screen keeps everything big and visual: the partner’s offer on top, yours at the bottom, and the three round buttons along the bottom edge where thumbs reach. Each squishy tile has a rarity stripe so kids can compare at a glance without reading.

How the market levels up

The market grows with the number of trades you finish. Higher levels add more stalls, a clock tower and lanterns, and unlock extras: a wishlist board (level 4), a "trade with friends" stall (level 6) and seasonal event stalls (level 8).

Building levels

From picnic blanket to grand bazaar

  1. Picnic swap. Two squishies trading on a blanket.
  2. Crate stall. A crate counter under an umbrella.
  3. Two tents. Striped market tents.
  4. Three tents. A third tent and bunting. Wishlist board unlocks.
  5. Market frame. A wooden frame goes up over the stalls.
  6. Market hall. A teal roof and SWAP MEET sign. Friends stall unlocks.
  7. Clock tower. A clock tower rises behind the roof.
  8. Lanterns. Lanterns, flowers and fence. Event stalls unlock.
  9. Glass dome. A glass dome tops the clock tower.
  10. Grand Bazaar. Golden dome tip, flags and medal.
