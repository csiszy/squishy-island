Cozy Teapot Kitchen

A giant butter-yellow teapot with polka dots, a spout that puffs steam and a handle that works as a garden arch. Home of the famous breakfast squishies.

  • Address: 6 Butter Boulevard
  • Unlocks: Dumpling Steamhouse reaches level 4
  • Family: 6 squishies
  • Levels: 1 (broken) to 10 (golden)

Try itDrag the slider under the picture to see the house at every level, with the whole family living there.

The family

The Pantry Pals

The breakfast crew, including the two most famous real-life squishies: the bendy banana and the viral butter block. When you get one from the Factory or the Market, it moves in here and appears around the house on the city map straight away.

Renovation

10 levels, from broken to golden

Each step costs coins, and some need family squishies living in the house. Tap any level to see it bigger with the family around it.

  1. Abandoned. Lid tipped over, spout snapped off, boarded windows.
  2. Clean-up. Tarp over the lid, cone and planks in the yard.
  3. Lid fixed. The lid sits back on with a new orange knob.
  4. Painted & spout. Fresh yellow paint, polka dots and a brand-new spout.
  5. Glass windows. Round windows with mint curtains.
  6. Orange door. Arched door, welcome mat and step.
  7. Garden. Picket fence, flower boxes and a stone path.
  8. Café awning. Striped awning over the door and a giant whisk & spoon fence post.
  9. Steam & lights. The spout puffs steam, fairy lights wrap the teapot.
  10. Golden Teapot. Golden knob, star medal and sparkles. Complete!

Inside the house

The Pantry Pals room

In the game, tapping the house on the city map opens this room. A mint-tiled kitchen-living room. Everything is a cooking tool: the bed is a toaster, the wardrobe is a fridge, the chandelier is a whisk. The numbers match the decor shop list below. Every decor item comes in 4 designs (Classic, Candy Pop, Mint Breeze and the fancier Starlight). Tap a design in the list to try it in the room, and drag the room to look around.

Life around the house

What you see on the city map

Banana pushes a little red lawnmower back and forth across the front lawn. Sunny Egg floats in a frying-pan paddling pool, Butter melts happily on a toast sunbed, Toast and Croissant pop up in the round windows, and the Pancake Tower glitters on the tip of the spout.

Every squishy has one fixed spot and one short looping action (2 to 4 seconds) that stays in place. A squishy only appears once you own it. If you trade it away, its spot becomes empty again.
