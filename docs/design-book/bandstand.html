Squeak Stage

The town’s music stage. It starts as two crates and a microphone, grows into a gazebo bandstand with curtains and spotlights, and ends as a big concert shell.

  • Address: 5 Cloudberry Hill
  • Unlocks: Coral Cove reaches level 3
  • Minigame: Squeak Beat (rhythm game with 30 tracks)
  • Play time: One song per track (1–1.5 min)

Try itDrag the slider to see the building at every level. Level 0 is the empty plot it starts as.

The minigame

Squeak Beat

▶ Play the prototypeWorks on phone and computer. Each minigame is one file you can open on its own: play/squeak-beat.html

A Piano Tiles style game with real songs. Your squishies themselves slide down four columns. Tap the lowest one (or anywhere in its column) any time while it is on screen: it just has to be its turn, with no other squishy below it. Long squishies trail a stripe you hold until it ends. Every tap plays the next note of the tune, so you are the one playing the melody.

The music: 30 different tracks, each one complete song (intro, verses, choruses, sometimes a breakdown, and an ending). Every track has its own style (pop, disco, swing, chill or march), tempo, key, chords and band, all made live in the browser: drums, bass, chords, bells, reverb and echo. The melody you play is written to fit the chords, and each band (Dumpling Gang, Sugar Squad, Sea Squish, Pet Pals) has its own voice.

How it plays

  1. Pick a track from the list. Each shows its difficulty, band, tempo and length.
  2. Squishies slide down four columns. The lowest one glows: that is the one to tap, on the squishy or anywhere in its column, as early as you like. Two side by side are both the next one.
  3. A squishy that slides off the bottom is a miss. Tapping a column whose squishy isn’t next, or letting go of a stripe too early, is a mistake. Every miss or mistake costs one of your 3 hearts; lose all 3 and the show stops.
  4. Tapping right as a squishy crosses the white line scores a PERFECT bonus, but it’s never required.
  5. Long squishies have a stripe behind them: tap and keep holding until the stripe has passed the line.

Controls

Tap or hold the four columns (two fingers for the harder tracks). PC: D F J K, 1 2 3 4 or the arrow keys. Sound on!

Scoring

  • Each squishy 200 points (300 for a PERFECT right on the line); held stripes add points while you hold. A long combo multiplies points (up to ×2).
  • Stars come from accuracy, the share of squishies you tapped: finish = ★, 85% = ★★, 95% = ★★★.
  • Best stars and best score per track are saved.

Stars open new tracks

  • Track N opens when you have (N − 1) × 3 stars in total: track 2 needs 3★, track 3 needs 6★, track 10 needs 27★. Every locked track shows how many stars it needs and how many you have.
  • Later tracks are faster (from about 86 to 160 BPM), have more notes per bar, more and longer held stripes, two squishies at once (chords) and a breakdown section.
  • Squishies scroll faster on harder tracks, so you have less time to read ahead.

Squishy helpers

You always play with squishies you own. Some families are extra good at this game:

  • Each family has its own squeak voice, so the band you bring changes the sound.
  • Sea Squish band members make the timing window a little wider.
  • A full band from one family gets a bonus encore track.

Rewards

About 40 coins per minute of music, times your accuracy (a 1:20 song at 90% pays about 48), so it pays like the other minigames. Replays pay the same way, so playing a favourite track again is never wasted. Every new building level unlocks a new set of tracks in the real game.

Keep going (optional ad)

When the run ends, the player can choose to watch a short ad once per run: finish the song with 1 heart (the next few squishies are skipped so you can get ready). “No thanks” ends the run as normal. See the ad rules.

Building levels

From empty plot to level 10

The plot is fixed on the map and shows a “Build me!” sign until you pay to build level 1. After that, each upgrade unlocks when you have cleared enough tracks in Squeak Beat, and then costs coins. Better results in the minigame = a fancier building. Tap any level to see it bigger.

  1. Crate stage. Two crates and a microphone.
  2. Speaker & bunting. A speaker and party flags.
  3. Wooden stage. A proper wooden platform.
  4. Awning. A purple striped awning on posts.
  5. Gazebo. A round bandstand roof on white posts.
  6. Painted & railed. Lilac paint and railings; the octopus joins the band.
  7. Speakers. Big speakers on both sides.
  8. Curtains & spotlights. Red curtains, spotlights and a taller roof.
  9. Music note sign. A giant bouncing music note and fairy lights.
  10. Concert Shell. A big arched shell behind the stage and sparkles.
