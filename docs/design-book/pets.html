Paw Palace

A giant dog kennel with a bone-shaped sign, a chimney and an attic window. The busiest, waggiest house in town.

  • Address: 1 Butter Boulevard
  • Unlocks: Teapot Kitchen reaches level 4
  • Family: 6 squishies
  • Levels: 1 (broken) to 10 (golden)

Try itDrag the slider under the picture to see the house at every level, with the whole family living there.

The family

The Pet Pals

Real-life pets made squishy. Every one of them has a pet-like habit in the city: sleeping on the roof, guarding the mail, napping on the doormat. When you get one from the Factory or the Market, it moves in here and appears around the house on the city map straight away.

Renovation

10 levels, from broken to golden

Each step costs coins, and some need family squishies living in the house. Tap any level to see it bigger with the family around it.

  1. Abandoned. Hole in the roof, bone sign hanging crooked, boarded windows.
  2. Clean-up. Tarp on the roof hole, cone and planks out front.
  3. New roof. Red roof tiles and the bone sign back in place.
  4. Fresh planks. Walls painted a warm biscuit colour.
  5. Glass windows. Windows with yellow curtains, attic window cleaned.
  6. Blue door. A big arched door (big enough for the Corgi).
  7. Garden. Fence, flower boxes, stepping stones.
  8. Paw path. Paw-print stepping stones and two lanterns by the roof.
  9. Party roof. String lights along both roof slopes.
  10. Golden Paw Palace. Star medal on the sign and sparkles. Complete!

Inside the house

The Pet Pals room

In the game, tapping the house on the city map opens this room. A sunny pet room with paw-print wallpaper, a tall cat tree, a donut dog bed and a hamster tube that runs across the wall. The numbers match the decor shop list below. Every decor item comes in 4 designs (Classic, Candy Pop, Mint Breeze and the fancier Starlight). Life around the house

What you see on the city map

Bunny mows the lawn, Puppy waits at the mailbox with a letter in his mouth, Kitty sleeps on the warm roof, Hamster pops in and out of the attic window, the Corgi Loaf snores on the doormat and the Lucky Cat waves non-stop from the roof peak.

Every squishy has one fixed spot and one short looping action (2 to 4 seconds) that stays in place. A squishy only appears once you own it. If you trade it away, its spot becomes empty again.
