Pineapple Orchard

A giant pineapple house with a spiky leaf crown. At level 1 the leaves are brown and droopy; by level 10 it’s the juiciest house on the hill.

  • Address: 6 Cloudberry Hill
  • Unlocks: Open from day one (starts broken; fix it up with coins)
  • Family: 6 squishies
  • Levels: 1 (broken) to 10 (golden)

Try itDrag the slider under the picture to see the house at every level, with the whole family living there.

The family

The Fresh Fruits

Juicy real-life fruits plus a magical golden apple from the oldest tree in town. When you get one from the Factory or the Market, it moves in here and appears around the house on the city map straight away.

Renovation

10 levels, from broken to golden

Each step costs coins, and some need family squishies living in the house. Tap any level to see it bigger with the family around it.

  1. Wilted. Brown droopy leaves, faded skin, boarded windows.
  2. Clean-up. Weeds cleared, planks and cone ready.
  3. Green crown. Fresh green leaves sprout from the top.
  4. Golden skin. Bright yellow skin with its pineapple pattern.
  5. Round windows. Glass and red curtains.
  6. Green door. A green arched door and mat.
  7. Garden. Fence, flowers and a stone path.
  8. Fruit crate porch. A crate of fruit and a watering can by the door.
  9. Lemon lanterns. Two lemon-shaped lanterns on a string.
  10. Golden Pineapple. Star medal and sparkles. Complete!

Inside the house

The Fresh Fruits room

In the game, tapping the house on the city map opens this room. A leafy orchard cottage with fruit-crate bunk beds, a juice bar and a hammock made from a melon rind. The numbers match the decor shop list below. Every decor item comes in 4 designs (Classic, Candy Pop, Mint Breeze and the fancier Starlight). Tap a design in the list to try it in the room, and drag the room to look around.

Cozy pointsEvery decor item adds Cozy points to the house. More Cozy means the family is happier: each 5 Cozy gives this family’s squishies +5% coins in minigames, and some items unlock extra idle animations.

Life around the house

What you see on the city map

Strawberry swings on a tyre swing under the yard tree, Avocado sunbathes on a sunbed, Peach and Watermelon peek out of the windows, Dragon Fruit sways in the leaf crown and the Golden Apple floats on the step.

Every squishy has one fixed spot and one short looping action (2 to 4 seconds) that stays in place. A squishy only appears once you own it. If you trade it away, its spot becomes empty again.
