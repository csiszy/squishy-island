Sweet Shoppe

A cupcake house with a striped wrapper, a frosting roof and a cherry on top. When it’s broken, the frosting has melted into a sad grey puddle.

  • Address: 1 Squeeze Street
  • Unlocks: Paw Palace reaches level 4
  • Family: 6 squishies
  • Levels: 1 (broken) to 10 (golden)

Try itDrag the slider under the picture to see the house at every level, with the whole family living there.

The family

The Sugar Squad

Sweets and desserts, from the squishiest marshmallow to a see-through rainbow gummy bear. When you get one from the Factory or the Market, it moves in here and appears around the house on the city map straight away.

Renovation

10 levels, from broken to golden

Each step costs coins, and some need family squishies living in the house. Tap any level to see it bigger with the family around it.

  1. Melted. The frosting has slumped and gone grey, windows boarded.
  2. Clean-up. Sticky mess scraped off, planks and cone out front.
  3. New frosting. A big fluffy swirl of pink frosting is piped back on.
  4. Sprinkles! Wrapper painted blue, sprinkles all over the roof.
  5. Cherry & windows. The cherry chimney is back and the windows get glass.
  6. Pink door. A pink arched door and mat.
  7. Garden. White fence, flower boxes and a stone path.
  8. Lollipop trees. Two giant lollipops guard the yard.
  9. Sweet lights. Fairy lights loop around the frosting edge.
  10. Golden Cupcake. Star medal and sparkles. Complete!

Inside the house

The Sugar Squad room

In the game, tapping the house on the city map opens this room. A candy-striped parlour with a cupcake sofa, lollipop lamps and a chocolate fountain that really pours. The numbers match the decor shop list below. Every decor item comes in 4 designs (Classic, Candy Pop, Mint Breeze and the fancier Starlight). Tap a design in the list to try it in the room, and drag the room to look around.

Life around the house

What you see on the city map

Donut floats in a milk paddling pool like a pool ring, Marshmallow bounces on a jelly trampoline, Macaron and Ice Cream Cone peek out of the windows, Strawberry Shortcake dances on the frosting and the Rainbow Gummy Bear bounces on the front step.

Every squishy has one fixed spot and one short looping action (2 to 4 seconds) that stays in place. A squishy only appears once you own it. If you trade it away, its spot becomes empty again.
