Cloud Nine

A made-up family’s home: a fluffy cloud on a stilt. At level 1 it’s a grey, deflated rain cloud dripping on the grass. From level 6 the whole house gently floats.

  • Address: 3 Cloudberry Hill
  • Unlocks: Open from day one (starts broken; fix it up with coins)
  • Family: 6 squishies
  • Levels: 1 (broken) to 10 (golden)

Try itDrag the slider under the picture to see the house at every level, with the whole family living there.

The family

The Sky Puffs

Made-up sky creatures: clouds, raindrops, a sun, a star, a moon and the rare Rainbow Puff. When you get one from the Factory or the Market, it moves in here and appears around the house on the city map straight away.

Renovation

10 levels, from broken to golden

Each step costs coins, and some need family squishies living in the house. Tap any level to see it bigger with the family around it.

  1. Deflated. A grey, droopy rain cloud with boarded windows.
  2. Clean-up. A tarp patch, ladder and planks.
  3. Dripping less. The cloud is still grey but the leaks are fixed.
  4. Puffed up! The cloud fluffs up big and white.
  5. Rope ladder. Glass windows and a rope ladder to the door.
  6. Balloons. Three balloons tied on. The house starts to float.
  7. Garden. Fence, flowers and a stone path.
  8. Rainbow slide. A rainbow arcs down from the cloud with cloud puffs at its feet.
  9. Starlight. Fairy lights and twinkling stars.
  10. Golden Cloud. Star medal and sparkles. Complete!

Inside the house

The Sky Puffs room

In the game, tapping the house on the city map opens this room. A lavender bedroom covered in stars, with a cloud bed, a moon lamp and a rainbow bookshelf. The floor is made of fluffy cloud. The numbers match the decor shop list below. Every decor item comes in 4 designs (Classic, Candy Pop, Mint Breeze and the fancier Starlight). Tap a design in the list to try it in the room, and drag the room to look around.

Life around the house

What you see on the city map

Puff Cloud drifts in the sky, Raindrop jumps in a puddle, Sunny sits on top of the cloud with rays slowly spinning, Star Blob peeks out of a window, Moon Mochi naps on a sunbed in a nightcap and the Rainbow Puff glides over the roof.

Every squishy has one fixed spot and one short looping action (2 to 4 seconds) that stays in place. A squishy only appears once you own it. If you trade it away, its spot becomes empty again.
