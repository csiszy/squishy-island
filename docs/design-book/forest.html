Mushroom Hollow

A giant red toadstool on the edge of the woods. At level 1 the cap is droopy, mossy and full of holes. At level 10 it glows with fireflies and fairy lights.

  • Address: 1 Cloudberry Hill (edge of the woods)
  • Unlocks: Open from day one (starts broken; fix it up with coins)
  • Family: 6 squishies
  • Levels: 1 (broken) to 10 (golden)

Try itDrag the slider under the picture to see the house at every level, with the whole family living there.

The family

The Forest Folk

A made-up woodland family: seeds, acorns, moss, little mushrooms, a mystery fluffball and a baby dragon. When you get one from the Factory or the Market, it moves in here and appears around the house on the city map straight away.

Renovation

10 levels, from broken to golden

Each step costs coins, and some need family squishies living in the house. Tap any level to see it bigger with the family around it.

  1. Rotting. A droopy, mossy cap with holes and boarded windows.
  2. Clean-up. A tarp over the hole, planks and cone.
  3. Firm cap. The cap is rebuilt round and sturdy.
  4. Red & spotty. Bright red cap with white spots.
  5. Windows. Glass windows with yellow curtains.
  6. Wooden door. A rounded door and mat.
  7. Garden. A rustic fence, flowers and stepping stones.
  8. Mini mushrooms. Tiny colourful mushrooms and two lanterns.
  9. Firefly night. Fireflies blink around the cap, fairy lights underneath.
  10. Golden Hollow. Star medal and sparkles. Complete!

Inside the house

The Forest Folk room

In the game, tapping the house on the city map opens this room. A log cabin room inside the stem, with a tree-stump fireplace, a leaf hammock and a firefly jar for a lamp. The numbers match the decor shop list below. Every decor item comes in 4 designs (Classic, Candy Pop, Mint Breeze and the fancier Starlight). Life around the house

What you see on the city map

Sprout waters itself with a tiny watering can, Moss Ball rolls back and forth on the lawn, Acorn and Fluffle peek out of windows, Shroomy dances on top of the cap and the Moss Dragon puffs flower petals from the cap edge.

Every squishy has one fixed spot and one short looping action (2 to 4 seconds) that stays in place. A squishy only appears once you own it. If you trade it away, its spot becomes empty again.
