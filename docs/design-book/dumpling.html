Dumpling Steamhouse

A tower of bamboo steamer baskets with a lid for a roof. It is the first house every player fixes up, so it teaches how renovating works.

  • Address: 7 Butter Boulevard
  • Unlocks: Free from the start (tutorial house)
  • Family: 6 squishies
  • Levels: 1 (broken) to 10 (golden)

Try itDrag the slider under the picture to see the house at every level, with the whole family living there.

The family

The Dumpling Gang

Six dumplings who love warm baths, naps and letters. Bao Bun is the tutorial squishy everyone gets for free on day one. When you get one from the Factory or the Market, it moves in here and appears around the house on the city map straight away.

Renovation

10 levels, from broken to golden

Each step costs coins, and some need family squishies living in the house. Tap any level to see it bigger with the family around it.

  1. Abandoned. Lid knocked sideways, windows boarded up, weeds, cobwebs and an old tyre.
  2. Clean-up. Weeds pulled, a blue tarp over the hole in the lid, planks piled up.
  3. New lid. The steamer lid sits straight again and gets its red knob back.
  4. Fresh bamboo. The walls are re-woven and turn golden again.
  5. Glass windows. Boards come off, curtains go up.
  6. Red front door. A shiny new door, welcome mat and step.
  7. Garden. Picket fence, flower boxes and a stepping-stone path.
  8. Chopstick arch. Giant chopsticks frame the door, red lanterns on both sides.
  9. Steamy nights. Fairy lights on the lid, glowing windows, steam puffing from the vent.
  10. Golden Steamhouse. Golden knob, star medal and sparkles. Complete!

Inside the house

The Dumpling Gang room

In the game, tapping the house on the city map opens this room. A cosy tatami room under a big round window. The lantern glows at night, and you can see that glow from the city map too. The numbers match the decor shop list below. Every decor item comes in 4 designs (Classic, Candy Pop, Mint Breeze and the fancier Starlight). Tap a design in the list to try it in the room, and drag the room to look around.

Life around the house

What you see on the city map

The front yard is where the soup-bowl pool lives: Bao Bun cannonballs in, pops out and jumps again in a three-second loop. Gyoza snores on a sunbed, Xiao Long Bao pops up in the top window, Har Gow reads mail on the step, Peach Bun sways on the lid and the Golden Dumpling floats above the knob.

Every squishy has one fixed spot and one short looping action (2 to 4 seconds) that stays in place. A squishy only appears once you own it. If you trade it away, its spot becomes empty again.
