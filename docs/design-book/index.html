Squishy Island

Collect squishies, fix up a tumbledown little town one house at a time, and play minigames in its shops. This map is the game’s home screen. Drag to explore, zoom in to see the squishies living there, and tap any building to open its design page. Every building, road and tree has a fixed place.

Try the minigames

Each minigame is a small playable version you can open on a phone or a computer. Five are endless: the longer you last, the harder they get, so there is always a better score to chase. Three are level-based (two puzzles and the ball breaker) with 1–3 stars per level and new levels forever, and Squeak Beat has 30 songs to unlock. The Squish Lab is a toy with no coins. Every game shows the coins you earned, the time played and your coin jar total at the end, and they all pay about the same per minute.
