Family house 6 of 8 · Sea Squish

Coral Cove

A giant pink conch shell on the sand. When you find it, it’s bleached grey and covered in barnacles and tangled seaweed.

  • Address: 7 Cloudberry Hill (the beach end)
  • Unlocks: Pineapple Orchard reaches level 4
  • Family: 6 squishies
  • Levels: 1 (broken) to 10 (golden)

Try itDrag the slider under the picture to see the house at every level, with the whole family living there.

The family

The Sea Squish

Sea creatures, from a wobbly jellyfish to an ancient turtle carrying a glowing pearl. When you get one from the Factory or the Market, it moves in here and appears around the house on the city map straight away.

Renovation

10 levels, from broken to golden

Each step costs coins, and some need family squishies living in the house. Tap any level to see it bigger with the family around it.

  1. Washed up. Bleached grey shell, barnacles and tangled seaweed.
  2. Clean-up. Seaweed cut away, planks and cone on the sand.
  3. Scrubbed. Barnacles scraped off the shell.
  4. Coral pink. The shell gets its pink stripes back.
  5. Portholes. Round porthole windows with sea-blue curtains.
  6. Blue door. A new door inside the shell’s opening.
  7. Beach garden. Sand path, driftwood fence and flowers.
  8. Beach day. A red-and-white beach umbrella and starfish on the sand.
  9. Bubbles. Floating bubbles and fairy lights.
  10. Golden Shell. Star medal and sparkles. Complete!

Inside the house

The Sea Squish room

In the game, tapping the house on the city map opens this room. An underwater-themed room with a porthole aquarium, a clam-shell bed with a pearl pillow and seaweed curtains. The numbers match the decor shop list below. Every decor item comes in 4 designs (Classic, Candy Pop, Mint Breeze and the fancier Starlight). Tap a design in the list to try it in the room, and drag the room to look around.

Life around the house

What you see on the city map

Axolotl paddles in a tide pool, Whale sunbathes on a beach towel, Jellyfish floats beside the shell tip, Pufferfish and Octopus peek out of the portholes, and the Pearl Turtle rests at the door with her pearl glowing.

Every squishy has one fixed spot and one short looping action (2 to 4 seconds) that stays in place. A squishy only appears once you own it. If you trade it away, its spot becomes empty again.
